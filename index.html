A few tips for a beautiful page

Colored pencils first

Soft colored pencils give that gentle, layered look. Gel pens and fine markers work too.

Use heavier paper

Printer paper is fine, but cardstock or 32 lb paper feels lovely and won’t buckle.

Start pale

Lay down light tones first, then build shadows. Ivory, powder blue and blush keep it airy.
