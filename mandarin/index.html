How to learn while you color

Trace the big character

The hero character is hollow. Fill it slowly, stroke by stroke, and say 茶 chá out loud as you go.

Color the labels last

Each word sits next to the thing it names. Color the teapot, then read 茶壶 cháhú. Your hand remembers.

Mind the tones

The little marks over the pinyin are tones. Chá rises, like asking a question. Get the tone right and the word is right.
